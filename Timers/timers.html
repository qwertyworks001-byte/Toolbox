Alarm sound & alerts

Browsers only allow sound after you’ve tapped the page, so Toolbox unlocks sound on your first tap on any Toolbox page and keeps it unlocked. Timers keep counting accurately even in a background tab. If your phone’s screen is off or the browser has put this tab fully to sleep, no website can wake it: that’s what notifications and “keep my screen awake” are for. Notifications work while Toolbox is open in a tab or the installed app.
