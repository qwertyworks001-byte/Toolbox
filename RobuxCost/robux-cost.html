How this works. Robux is sold in fixed packs, so you usually can’t buy an exact amount. Given a number of Robux, this finds the cheapest set of packs that gives at least that many. Given a budget, it finds the set of packs that gives the most Robux without going over. Roblox says buying on the web, on a computer or with a gift card gives up to 25% more Robux than the mobile app or console (Roblox Support). The Premium 10% purchase bonus ended on 30 May 2026, and Roblox Plus doesn’t add bonus Robux when you buy, so neither is included. Pack sizes and US prices were checked on ; Roblox can change them at any time. “Estimate from US prices” is only an estimate: Roblox and the app stores set their own prices in each country (usually including tax), so what you’re actually charged can be noticeably different. For exact results, choose “Use my store’s prices” and type in what your store shows. Selling Robux instead of buying? See the DevEx Calculator. Exchange rates by Exchange Rate API.
