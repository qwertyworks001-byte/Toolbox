Fancy Text & Discord Fonts

Type once, get your text in dozens of styles. Click any result to copy it, then paste it into Discord, Roblox, Instagram or anywhere else.

Fonts and effects are special Unicode characters, so they work almost anywhere you can paste text, but a few apps or fonts may show boxes for some styles. Screen readers also read them poorly, so keep important info in plain text. “Discord formatting” uses Discord’s own markdown and only looks styled inside Discord.
