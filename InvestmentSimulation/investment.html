Enter one expected average annual return (e.g. 10% for something like the S&P 500, or 4% for a more conservative asset) and choose a volatility profile. Best and Worst case rates are then derived automatically around that average — wider for higher-volatility assets, narrower for stable ones — and compounded yearly against your lump sum plus contributions. This is a simplified illustrative model, not financial advice.

How It’s Calculated

Amounts are treated as being in whichever currency is selected; nothing is converted. Use an inflation rate as the discount rate to see future money in today’s purchasing power. This is a simplified illustrative model, not financial advice.
