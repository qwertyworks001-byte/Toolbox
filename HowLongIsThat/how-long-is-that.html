How Long Is That?

Turn a big confusing number of time units into something readable.

11 days, 13 hours, 46 minutes, 40 seconds

Exact — fixed-length units only, no calendar assumptions.

1 year, 4 months, 15 days

Approximate — months don't have a fixed number of days, so this uses an average (30.44 days/month, 365.25 days/year).
